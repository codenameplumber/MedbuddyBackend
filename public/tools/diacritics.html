What the font audit found Twenty characters are missing from every bundled brand font (IBM Plex, Fraunces). Yorùbá and Igbo dot-below vowels sit in the Vietnamese Unicode subset the bundled files skip; the Hausa hooked letters simply have no glyphs in Plex. So the patient app uses a system stack instead — that is what the multilingual rows below are testing.

Tone marks stacked on dotted vowels

The hardest case: a combining acute or grave sitting on a vowel that already carries a dot below. If the marks collide, overlap, or the dot disappears, the stack is wrong.
